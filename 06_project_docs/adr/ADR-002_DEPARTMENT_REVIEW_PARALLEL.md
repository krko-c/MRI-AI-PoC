# ADR-002 — Department Review as a Parallel Internal Evidence Lane

- Date: 2026-08-18
- Status: ACCEPTED

## Decision

Department Review is a parallel internal-evidence lane that starts after Screening.

It is not a late-stage confirmation step after Strategy.

Relevant departments independently review the screened MRI issue and provide:
- current activities,
- possible actions / ideas,
- future plans,
- constraints.

Department responses are one source within Company / Internal State and must remain distinguishable from:
- internal system facts,
- AI inference,
- external evidence.

Department Review may proceed while BO, Market / Competitor Intelligence, and Company State Research are running.

It must not flow backward into initial BO generation.
